FAQ

Common questions

Setup varies a little between devices. The short guides below cover the three most popular routes.

Still stuck? Resize an image first, then follow the guide for your model.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All cropping and resizing happens locally in your browser using the canvas API. Your images are never transmitted to or stored on any server, and no account is required. Nothing is retained after you close the tab — and once the page is loaded, the tool keeps working even offline.

How do I set up custom screensavers on Kindle?

Kindle doesn't officially support custom screensavers, but a common workaround uses a book cover that is actually your image:

  1. Resize your image here so it matches your Kindle model's dimensions.
  2. Convert the PNG into an EPUB file with the image embedded as its cover (Calibre or an online converter works).
  3. Send the EPUB to your Kindle via Send to Kindle.
  4. On the device, enable Settings → Device Options → Display Cover.
  5. Sync, open the “book”, and put your Kindle to sleep — your image appears on the lockscreen.

This is a community workaround, and its effectiveness may vary by model and firmware.

How do I set up custom screensavers on Kobo?
  1. Connect your Kobo to a computer via USB and tap Connect on the device.
  2. Open the Kobo drive and go into the .kobo folder.
  3. Create a new folder named exactly screensaver, and copy your downloaded images into it.
  4. Safely eject, then open Settings → Energy saving and privacy.
  5. Make sure Show current read and Show book covers full screen are both on, then put your Kobo to sleep.
How do I set up custom screensavers in KOReader?
  1. Connect your e-reader via USB and open the .adds/koreader folder on the device.
  2. Create a folder such as screensaver and copy your images into it.
  3. Safely eject, then in KOReader open Settings → Screen → Sleep screen → Wallpaper.
  4. Choose a single custom image or a random folder, and point it at your new folder.
What does this e-reader image resizer do?

It resizes and positions images for e-reader screens. Pick your device, upload a photo, adjust the crop, and the tool outputs the image at the device's exact pixel dimensions and aspect ratio — so it always displays without stretching, cropping, or black bars.

Why should I resize images for my e-reader?

E-readers use specific screen dimensions and aspect ratios. Properly sized images display sharply and fill the screen edge to edge — which matters for cover images, wallpapers, and screensaver art. Off-size images get stretched, cropped, or show unwanted borders.
